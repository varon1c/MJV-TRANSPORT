Privacy Notice

MJV Transport uses the information you submit to respond to booking and inquiry requests or to assess a driver application.

Information we collect

Depending on the form, we collect your name, contact details, trip or inquiry information, and application details. Driver applications also require a photo, an identity document, and a driver's license.

How information is used and protected

Information is stored in the application's PostgreSQL database. Applicant documents are stored separately in a private server directory and are available only to authenticated administrators. The site does not publish applicant documents or use submitted information for unrelated marketing.

Retention and deletion

Applications marked rejected or hired, together with their uploaded documents, are scheduled for deletion 180 days after their last status update. Applications still under review are not automatically deleted. Deletion runs as a documented maintenance task; encrypted backups may retain deleted data until those backups expire under the operator's backup schedule.

Booking and inquiry records are retained only as needed to respond and operate the service, subject to applicable legal and accounting requirements; their deletion schedule must be set by the service operator. Ask MJV Transport to correct or delete information by using the contact form or the contact details listed there. Some records may need to be retained where the law requires it.
